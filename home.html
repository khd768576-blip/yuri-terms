FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Korean, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai, Filipino and Traditional Chinese. All explanations and translations are shown in the language you choose.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I start?

Yes. Start with hiragana and katakana, then move up level by level from N5 to N1.

Do I need an account?

No. You can study without signing in. Sign in with Google only if you want weekly leagues, friends and a backup of your progress.

Is there an iPhone version?

The app is on Android for now. An iPhone version is in preparation.

Is this an official JLPT app?

No. We use the JLPT levels to organise what you learn, but the app is not affiliated with the Japan Foundation or JEES.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings › My account › Delete account. Everything is removed right away. You can also request deletion by email.
