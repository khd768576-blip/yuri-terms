========================================================================

ATTRIBUTION SUMMARY

Vocabulary lists (N5–N1): based on Jonathan Waller's JLPT resources
(tanos.co.uk, CC BY), compiled in open-anki-jlpt-decks by Jamie Sinclair
(MIT License).

This app includes material from the JMdict and KANJIDIC2 dictionary files in
accordance with the licence provisions of the Electronic Dictionary Research
and Development Group (EDRDG) — https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
(CC BY-SA 4.0). Kanji data via kanji-data by David Gouveia (MIT License) and
jmdict-simplified by Dmitry Shpika. The data were shortened and edited for
this app; the edited word and kanji data are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Stroke order: based on KanjiVG © Ulrich Apel (kanjivg.tagaini.net),
CC BY-SA 3.0; stroke paths were simplified and the modified stroke data is
licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Illustrations: Fluent Emoji, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation,
MIT License (recolored).

Topic icons: Lucide, Copyright (c) Lucide Icons and Contributors, ISC License.

Fonts: Pretendard by Kil Hyung-jin (subset, renamed YuriSans) and Noto Color
Emoji by Google (subset, bundled as AppEmoji), SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Voices of Yuri and Ken are AI-generated (ElevenLabs).

Grammar points, example sentences and translations were written for
Japanese with Yuri. Korean kanji readings (hun-eum) were checked against the
libhangul hanja table (Copyright (c) 2005, 2006 Choe Hwanjin, BSD licence).

"JLPT" refers to the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test. This app is not
affiliated with or endorsed by the Japan Foundation or JEES. The JLPT has not
published official vocabulary or kanji lists since 2010; these are widely
used study lists.
